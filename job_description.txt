About the Role
The Data Engineering Team designs, builds, and oversees the deployment of technology architecture and software to capture, manage, store, and utilize structured and unstructured data. You will help establish processes based on business and technical requirements, channel data from multiple inputs, and suggest system upgrades to accommodate ongoing needs. This is a paid, full-time internship with the potential for post-internship career opportunities.

Key Responsibilities
- Develop technical tools and programming that leverage AI, Machine Learning, and big-data techniques to cleanse, organize, and transform data.
- Maintain, defend, and update data structures and integrity on an automated basis.
- Create and manage declarative automations and data validation to support business processes.
- Partner directly with business stakeholders to gather requirements and translate business needs into technical data solutions.
- Design and develop innovative applications that leverage AI and ML to drive automated insights.
- Follow best practices to produce clean, efficient, and maintainable data pipelines and configurations.
- Build advanced monitoring and alerting frameworks (Observability) that utilize AI to predict and resolve pipeline failures.

What You Will Learn
- How to leverage AI and Machine Learning in data engineering workflows.
- Strategies for partnering with stakeholders to deliver technical solutions.
- Best practices for designing scalable data platforms and infrastructure.
- How to build self-service tools for engineering teams.
- Methods for automating the lifecycle of data pipeline creation.

Focus Areas (You may work on one or more of these)
- Data Platform & Enablement: Building and scaling the underlying infrastructure that powers the Data Cloud.
- AI-Driven Automation: Designing intelligent solutions to replace manual tasks in data pipeline creation.
- Observability & Operations: Building monitoring frameworks to predict and resolve failures.
- Data Engineering Excellence: Refining data architectures to ensure high-quality data flows across the ecosystem.
